BUG-CONTENT-01 — FIXED (was HIGH)

FAQ answer text did not match the ticket brief

An earlier revision of v2.js shipped a different closing sentence for the new FAQ answer ("The providers featured on Renters Insurance Gurus offer policies designed to meet common landlord insurance requirements.") than the brief specified. The re-supplied v2.js now matches the brief verbatim, including the brief's own double space before "across", and also dropped the earlier <b>...</b> bold wrapper around the middle sentence. Verified fixed on all 3 target pages, all 7 browsers.

BUG-A — MEDIUM–HIGH (open, inherited)

scrollToEl() does not account for prior scroll position

Same root cause as SWF137/CRE-T-144 BUG-01/A on the sister site: top = el.getBoundingClientRect().top - 100 omits window.scrollY, so the target is only correct when the click happens from scrollY=0. Reproduced on all 7 browsers: a click from scrollY=0 lands correctly, but a click after any prior page scroll lands the FAQ header far from the intended ~0-100px offset. Fix: var top = window.scrollY + rect.top - 100;
