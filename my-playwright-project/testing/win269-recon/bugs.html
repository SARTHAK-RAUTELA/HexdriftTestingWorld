HighBUG-02: 120-Night Home Trial and Lifetime Warranty land in the wrong place on the first click

scrollToElement() computes the target Y once, then smooth-scrolls. While the page scrolls, content above these two targets changes height: #shopify-section-shop-box 550→0, and WIN248's .cre-t-248-section is injected and grows 261→810px. The browser ends at a stale Y.

  • Trial: missed on every fresh-load click in Chrome recon (4/4), with the section top at −111 to −372px. The trial heading is hidden, and the user sees the end of the trial section and the warranty promise. Waiting 20s after load before clicking still missed 3/3.
  • Warranty: lands 150–280px short (top 309–439) or overshoots (−372).
  • A second click, once the page has settled, lands correctly (top 160), which confirms it's reflow during the first scroll.
  • Per browser (fresh-load run): Trial missed on Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Warranty missed on Chrome, Edge and Firefox and landed on Safari.
  • Safari, Free Shipping & Returns: it landed correctly on the opened question (top 161), then about 2s later the page reflowed and left the user at the footer (top −841). Firefox landed 260px low, but the opened answer stayed fully visible, which is acceptable.

U.S.A. and Rating sit above the reflowing sections and land correctly. Showrooms already has a one-shot re-check, and Shipping waits 350ms before measuring. Fix: after the smooth scroll ends, re-measure and correct until the target's top is stable, for example re-checking at 600/1200/2000ms. The same issue is open on WIN282 (mobile), so both should share the fix.

MediumBUG-03: 1001–1439px renders 2 links per row (3 rows) instead of the wireframe's 3 per row

va3.css uses grid-template-columns: repeat(2, auto) and switches to 3 columns only at min-width:1440px. At 1280/1366 the bar is 565px wide, and the 3-column content needs about 536px, so 3 per row fits. 1280–1439 covers most laptop traffic. The reading order also changes: row 2 becomes Showrooms / Warranty. Unless agreed with the client, use 3 columns from about 1200px and keep 2 columns for 1001–1199, where it really doesn't fit (434px bar at 1024).

MediumBUG-06: 150+ Showrooms lands on the Trial section when WIN248's store locator isn't on the page (seen on Safari)

Showrooms scrolls to the bottom of #shopify-section-shop-frames minus 100px, which is where WIN248 injects “Try The WinkBed In Person”. In the Safari (WebKit) run, that section hadn't loaded and wasn't found during the 7s window. This is the same issue as WIN282 BUG-02, where the client approved /pages/store-locator as the fallback. Fix: target .cre-t-248-section directly, and if it's missing, navigate to /pages/store-locator.

LowBUG-04: keyboard activation (Enter) scrolls but fires no goal

Goals are bound to mousedown, so keyboard users' clicks aren't counted, even though the scroll works. Fix: fire goals from the existing click capture listener, which also fires on Enter, and drop the mousedown listener.

LowBUG-05: icon/text columns don't line up in the 2-column layout

At 1001–1439px the left-column labels start up to 4px apart, and in the right column “Free Shipping & Returns” starts about 17px right of “Lifetime Warranty” and “Rated 4.8 stars”, because the icon box widths differ (truck 36px, shield 25px, star 17px). Also, .cre-t-269-link { font-size:14px } in the 1001–1279 query has no effect, because .cre-t-269-text sets 15px directly.
